HELP / CAMPUSCODE GUIDE

STUDENT GUIDE.

Everything you need to navigate CampusCode, from joining a hackathon to completing your final round.

YOUR PLAYBOOK

JOIN. BUILD. SUBMIT. ADVANCE.

Follow the journey in order. Your dashboard and round pages will tell you what is currently available.

01 GET STARTED

Use your Dashboard to see your current activity, notifications and available hackathons.

02 JOIN A HACKATHON

Open Hackathons, review the event information and use JOIN HACKATHON when you are eligible.

03 BUILD YOUR TEAM

After joining, open My Team to create a team or use HackMate AI to discover compatible participants.

04 SUBMIT YOUR IDEA

When Round 1 is live, your team submits its problem statement. A Participant Agreement appears before the official submission.

05 ROUND 1 — ROUND 3

Watch the round status in each workspace. A later round can remain locked until the organizer workflow opens it for your team.

06 CHECK RESULTS

Use Results and Leaderboard to view published outcomes, scores and available organizer feedback.

07 USE AI RESPONSIBLY

IdeaCheck helps you review an idea. RuleBot helps with hackathon rules. Always treat the organizer's official instructions as authoritative.

DO THIS

WHAT TO DO

  • ✓ Read the hackathon details before joining.
  • ✓ Create or join the correct team for the hackathon.
  • ✓ Check your round status before preparing a submission.
  • ✓ Keep your project information accurate and consistent.
  • ✓ Read official announcements and deadlines.
  • ✓ Use IdeaCheck and RuleBot as supporting tools.
AVOID THIS

WHAT NOT TO DO

  • × Do not submit copied or unauthorized work.
  • × Do not share account credentials with other people.
  • × Do not wait until the last moment to check a round.
  • × Do not treat AI output as an official organizer decision.
  • × Do not ignore organizer announcements.
  • × Do not submit to the wrong hackathon or team.
FAQ

COMMON QUESTIONS

Can I submit Round 1 without a team? −
No. The current StudentPanel requires you to be part of a team before Round 1 submission.
Why is my round locked? +
Rounds unlock automatically once the previous round is evaluated by jury mentors or when the scheduled round start time is reached.
Where should I look for official communication? +
Follow the "Official Communication" tab in your Student Workspace and join the official WhatsApp channel for critical deadline alerts.
Can RuleBot replace organizer instructions? +
No. RuleBot helps guide you through common questions, but direct announcements from organizers and jury members always take absolute precedence.
